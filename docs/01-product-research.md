# Product Research

## 1. Problem

People often own many clothes but still struggle to decide what to wear. Their wardrobe exists physically, but they don't have an easy way to see, organize, and use it digitally.

## 2. Existing Solutions

Existing digital wardrobe and outfit-planning applications provide features such as:

* Digital wardrobe management
* Outfit creation
* Outfit recommendations
* Clothing categorization
* Outfit planning

However, many existing solutions have limitations in personalization, context-aware recommendations, and simplicity.

## 3. Opportunity

DreamCloset aims to combine digital wardrobe management with personalized outfit planning.

The application can eventually consider factors such as:

* The user's existing clothes
* Personal style
* Weather
* Location
* Occasion
* Previous outfits

## 4. Initial Goal

The initial goal of DreamCloset is to build an MVP that allows users to digitally organize their wardrobe and create or receive useful outfit combinations from the clothes they already own.
